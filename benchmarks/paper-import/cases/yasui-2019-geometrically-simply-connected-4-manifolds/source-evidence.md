# Source evidence (not a mathematical sign-off)

- Source PDF: 文章1.pdf (Kouichi Yasui, published 13 October 2019).
- Page 1: Theorem 1.4 states that every geometrically simply connected, positive definite, closed, smooth 4-manifold with b2+ greater than 1 has vanishing stable cohomotopy Seiberg–Witten invariant.
- Pages 4–5: the paper defines classes represented by 2-handle neighborhoods and states the corresponding vanishing theorem.
- Page 7: the paper states the obstruction theorem for embedded spheres under nonvanishing stable cohomotopy SW invariant.

The extracted source also confirms Theorem 1.9, Theorems 2.3, 2.4, 2.7, 2.8, and 2.9, Lemma 2.6, and the proof passage deriving Theorems 1.4 and 1.9. Theorem 1.4 itself is only the stable-cohomotopy vanishing statement; the symplectic consequence is downstream and must not be merged into that theorem entry. Theorem 2.9 has the precise closed/connected/oriented hypotheses and the nonzero restriction map condition. Full mathematical sign-off remains pending.
