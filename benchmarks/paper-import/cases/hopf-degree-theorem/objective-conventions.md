# Objective standard profile — gold-v2

This case has an objective mathematical standard at the level of invariants and hypotheses; equivalent wording is accepted.

## Fixed ambient conventions

- All spheres/disks use standard orientations, and maps are smooth unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- Degree is for a map between closed, connected, oriented manifolds of the same dimension; it is computed at a regular value by the signed preimage count and is independent of that regular value.
- The Hopf classification is for `k≥1` and self-maps of `S^k`; the free homotopy statement and based `π_k(S^k)` consequence are distinguished.
- The vector-field application is for compact smooth manifolds without boundary; isolated zero indices use the induced boundary-sphere orientation.

## Semantic invariants

- Degree classifies homotopy classes of self-maps of `S^k`.
- Degree zero is equivalent to null-homotopy and disk extension in the stated setting.
- Poincaré–Hopf plus local cancellation gives a nowhere-zero vector field exactly when `χ(M)=0`.
